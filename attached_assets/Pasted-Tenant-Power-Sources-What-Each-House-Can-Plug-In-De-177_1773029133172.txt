Key Rule of the Grid
Every single device above gets its own private power meter (tenant_id) the moment it connects.
The AI repair crew (your 4 Agents) works inside that house only.
You (super_admin) can still see the city-wide view, but no tenant can ever see another tenant’s laptop, phone, OneDrive, or rack.
Are You Going to Be the “Smart Guy” Who Handles Integrations and Glue?
Short answer: YES — but only for the first 5–10 customers.
After that, NO — the platform becomes self-serve and you step back to pure grid operator.
Here’s exactly how it plays out:
Phase 1 (Now – Next 30–60 days)
You are the smart guy.

You personally help the first 3–5 pilot tenants plug in their OneDrive, AWS account, or laptop.
You run the 2-minute connection scripts.
You verify the tenant sees only their house.
This is intentional: you learn real-world edge cases and make the onboarding bulletproof.
